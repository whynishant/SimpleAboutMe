I grew up writing stories, and I am currently pursuing my engineering in Noida, India.

I started making videos on YouTube back in 2020 when I received my first smartphone. Because of device limitations and low video quality, I had to step back for a while. Once I entered my first year of engineering, I picked up the camera again with fresh vision and started @nishantsjourney.

I have been writing stories and personal blogs ever since 10th grade. Telling stories is how I make sense of the world, discover structure in chaos, and document what it feels like to grow.

I am deeply curious about honing my craft, exploring the intersection of creative writing and engineering, and remaining a student for life.
