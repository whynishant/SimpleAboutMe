======================================================================
ASSETS FOLDER
======================================================================

You can place your local photography and book covers here:

Suggested structure:
- assets/me.jpg (for your author bio or avatar)
- assets/photo-1.jpg to photo-8.jpg (for your photo gallery)
- assets/books/mastery.jpg (for book covers)
- assets/og-banner.jpg (for Twitter/OpenGraph SEO cards)

Update the <img> 'src' attributes in the HTML files from the placeholder 
Unsplash URLs to your local filenames (e.g., src="assets/photo-1.jpg").
